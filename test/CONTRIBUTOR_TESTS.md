Each added invariant campaign uses 256 sequences of up to 80 calls, explicitly targets its handler selectors, and fails on unexpected reverts. Expected rejection paths assert their errors inside the handlers. The three new stateless fuzz properties use 1,000 runs each. Settings are inline in Solidity so default `forge test` retains them.

The prior LendingPool rounding finding has been fixed in the accepted source. The pool campaign now requires every actor's full share balance to be redeemable after debt settlement and complete vesting, then actually redeems it and checks that no shares or withdrawal entitlements remain. Regressions pin fee/donation vesting and retirement of unvested income at the last-share exit, including a new deposit and donation in the same timestamp. Donations without shareholders must revert; fees received without shareholders enter reserve. The ledger derives reserve reclassification from tracked cash flows, independently of the pool's reserve getter.
